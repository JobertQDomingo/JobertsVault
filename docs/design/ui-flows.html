Screens and flows

The visual version of UI_FLOWS.md, in the Glassmorphism 2.0 style: every Phase 1 screen as a desktop mockup, the 10 key flows, and outlines of the Phase 2–4 screens. The sidebar, top bar, period bar and cards are frosted glass panels floating over the aurora. Dialogs, menus and toasts use the stronger raised glass. The sample data is made up but adds up: the October totals, balances, chart bars and budgets all match each other.

§11 Decided

  • First launch: a plain setup form in Phase 1 (ONB-1), polished in Phase 4 (ONB-2).
  • Duplicate: the copy uses today's date (TXN-2).
  • Pay bill: suggests the full amount owed, editable (CC-9).
  • Window: fixed 1440 × 900, not resizable or maximizable. It fits both monitors (1920 × 1080 and 2560 × 1080) at 100% scaling and falls back to 1280 × 800, with the same layout zoomed to 88.9%, if the screen is too small.
  • Logo: C · Line Door, in the sidebar and the title bar.
